Livgreen LG 2.688KWH 25.6V 105Ah Inverter Battery

  • 25.6 V
  • 105 Ah
  • 2.688 kWh
  • LiFePO4
  • 60 months warranty

The Livgreen LG 2.688KWH is a 25.6V 105Ah lithium ferrophosphate (LiFePO4) battery with 2.688 kWh of energy, built by Revolt Lithium Energy Solutions in Muzaffarnagar for 24V home, office and solar inverters. It gives about 4.5 hours at a 500 W load and 2.2 hours at 1000 W, has a smart BMS with six protections and carries a 60-month warranty.

For 24V home and office inverters (about 1.5–2 kVA).

Common questions

What is the warranty on the Livgreen LG 2.688KWH?

60 months from purchase, backed by Revolt Lithium Energy Solutions, Muzaffarnagar. Keep the serial number on the pack for claims.

How long will the Livgreen LG 2.688KWH run my inverter?

About 4.5 hours at 500 W (roughly 5 ceiling fans and a few LED lights) and 2.2 hours at 1000 W, using 90% of the energy through a 92% efficient inverter.

What is the price of the Livgreen LG 2.688KWH?

Price depends on quantity and your city. Request a quotation on this page and it is emailed to you, or call 97560 73477.

Is the Livgreen LG 2.688KWH safe?

Yes. LiFePO4 cells are thermally stable and spill proof, and the BMS protects against over-voltage, over-discharge, short circuit and high temperature.
