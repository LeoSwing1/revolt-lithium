Lithium vs lead-acid e-rickshaw battery: the 3-year cost

By the Revolt Lithium team, Muzaffarnagar · Updated 2026-11-20

A lithium pack costs more on the first day. The question every driver asks is whether it pays back. Here is the full 3-year sum for a typical e-rickshaw in western Uttar Pradesh, using the same numbers as our savings calculator.

The assumptions

  • 90 km a day, 26 working days a month
  • Electricity at ₹7.5 per unit
  • A 4 × 12V lead-acid set at ₹30,000 that lasts about 12 months in daily use
  • A 51.2V 105Ah LiFePO4 pack at an example price of ₹62,000 (ask us for today's price)
  • About 50 Wh per km. Lithium charges at about 95% efficiency, lead-acid at about 75%, and the 120 kg lead-acid set adds roughly 8% to energy use.

The 3-year total

Cost over 36 monthsLead-acidLithium
Batteries₹90,000 (3 sets)₹62,000 (1 pack)
Charging electricity₹45,490₹33,253
Water top-up and upkeep₹7,200₹0
Total₹1,42,690₹95,253

At these numbers lithium saves about ₹47,000 in three years, roughly ₹1,300 a month. The pack is also under warranty for the full 36 months, while a lead-acid set is usually covered for 6 to 12 months.

What the table does not show

The lithium pack weighs 44 kg against about 120 kg for lead-acid. Less weight means easier climbs, less wear on tyres and suspension, and more room for passengers. It charges in 4 to 5 hours instead of 8 to 10, so a driver can top up at lunch. There is no acid to spill and no lead to scrap.

Your numbers will differ. Enter your own daily kilometres, electricity rate and the quotation you received in the calculator. If you run fewer than 40 km a day, the saving is smaller; above 100 km it grows quickly.

Which pack to choose

Both the Redtuff RT-5.376KWH and the Livgreen 51.2V 105Ah use the same 16S1P prismatic LFP cells, BMS and warranty. Choose the brand your area knows.

Questions

Is a lithium e-rickshaw battery worth it?

For daily use above about 50 km, yes. At 90 km a day the 3-year saving is around ₹47,000 compared with lead-acid, before counting the lower weight and faster charging.

Do I need a new charger for lithium?

Yes, use a lithium-compatible charger set for the 51.2V LFP pack. Our team checks your charger and controller before fitting.
