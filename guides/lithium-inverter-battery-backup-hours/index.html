Lithium inverter battery backup: how many hours will I get?

By the Revolt Lithium team, Muzaffarnagar · Updated 2026-11-19

Backup depends on two things: how much energy the battery stores, and how many watts you run during a power cut. Here is how to work it out in a minute.

The formula

Backup hours = battery kWh × 1000 × 0.9 × 0.92 ÷ load in watts. The 0.9 keeps 10% in reserve to protect the cells, and 0.92 is a typical inverter's efficiency. Our backup calculator does this for you.

What is 500 W in a home?

A ceiling fan uses about 75 W, an LED tube about 18 W, a TV 60 to 100 W, and a fridge about 150 W while its compressor runs. Five fans, six LED lights and a TV come to roughly 500 W.

Match the voltage to your inverter

  • 12V inverter (up to about 900 VA): 12.8V 1.344 kWh
  • 24V inverter (about 1.5 to 2 kVA): 25.6V 2.688 kWh, Redtuff or Livgreen
  • 48V inverter or solar hybrid (about 3 to 5 kVA): 48V 5.040 kWh, Redtuff or Livgreen
Lithium vs tubular: no acid, no water top-up, no fumes indoors, faster recharge after the power returns, and a 60-month warranty.

Questions

Can I use a lithium battery with my old inverter?

Yes, if the system voltage matches (12V, 24V or 48V). Set the inverter to a lithium or suitable charging profile; we can help on call.

How long does a lithium inverter battery last?

LiFePO4 is built for thousands of cycles, and our inverter batteries carry a 60-month warranty.
