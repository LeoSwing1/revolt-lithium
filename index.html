Engineering & technology

What's inside every pack

LiFePO4 cells, a battery management system that watches every cell, and a build process you can trace back by serial number.

LiFePO4 chemistry

Lithium ferrophosphate cells are thermally stable, spill proof and built for long cycle life.

Battery management system

Every pack has a BMS that tracks each cell and switches off before damage.

Voltage & current protection

Over-voltage, over-discharge and short-circuit protection on every pack.

Temperature monitoring

Charging pauses and power is limited when cells get too hot.

Tested before dispatch

Packs are checked on a battery management system tester at our facility.

Serial-number traceability

A laser-marked serial (like RLES24M060J00508) links each pack to its warranty.

For a long battery life

  • Use only the recommended lithium charger
  • Store between 14°C and 36°C when not in use
  • Charge in the shade, between 0°C and 55°C
  • Call us for any warning light or app alert

Never do this

  • Do not open or disassemble the pack
  • Do not leave it in direct sunlight
  • Keep it away from water
  • Do not dispose of it in fire

Warranty claims need these instructions to be followed.

FAQ

Common questions

Still unsure? Call 97560 73477 and talk to the team that builds the packs.

Do you make lithium inverter and solar batteries?

Yes. Livgreen and Redtuff LiFePO4 inverter batteries come in 12.8V (1.344 kWh), 25.6V (2.688 kWh) and 48V (5.040 kWh), all 105Ah with a 60-month warranty. They work with home, office and solar hybrid inverters.

How much backup will a lithium inverter battery give?

At a 500 W load (about 5 fans and a few lights), the 2.688 kWh battery gives about 4.5 hours and the 5.040 kWh battery about 8 hours. Use the inverter backup calculator for your own load.

What warranty do Livgreen and Redtuff batteries have?

E-rickshaw and e-2-wheeler packs carry a full 36-month (3-year) warranty and inverter batteries a 60-month (5-year) warranty, backed by Revolt Lithium Energy Solutions, Muzaffarnagar. Keep the serial number on the pack for claims.

Are Livgreen and Redtuff made by the same company?

Yes. Both brands are made, tested and serviced by Revolt Lithium Energy Solutions in Muzaffarnagar, with the same LFP cells, BMS and warranty.

How long does the 51.2V 105Ah e-rickshaw battery take to charge?

About 4 to 5 hours from low to full at the standard 20 A charging current. The pack accepts up to 25 A.

How many kilometres does the e-rickshaw battery run on one charge?

The 5.376 kWh pack gives roughly 90 to 100 km on a level road with normal load. Full passengers, slopes and tyre pressure change the figure.

Can I replace the lead-acid batteries in my e-rickshaw with lithium?

Yes. The 51.2V pack replaces a 48V lead-acid set (4 × 12V) in most e-rickshaws. Use a lithium-compatible charger; our team checks your controller and charger before fitting.

Which battery fits my electric scooter?

Match your scooter's voltage: 48V, 60V or 72V. We make 48V 30Ah, 60V 30Ah, 60V 32Ah and 72V 30Ah LiFePO4 packs. Send your scooter model in the quotation form and we will confirm the right one.

Why is LiFePO4 safer than other lithium batteries?

Lithium ferrophosphate cells are thermally stable and spill proof. Every pack also has a BMS with over-voltage, over-discharge, short-circuit and temperature protection.

Can I check my battery's health on my phone?

Yes, on Revolt RLES packs and on Bluetooth-enabled packs. Scan the QR code on the pack to install the BMS app and see each cell's voltage and temperature.

What is the price of a lithium e-rickshaw battery?

Price depends on the model, quantity and your city. Fill the quotation form to get the quotation on email, or call 97560 73477.
