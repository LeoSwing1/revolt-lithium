Quotations

Quotations sent from this website are valid for 7 days from the date shown on the quotation. Prices are subject to GST and to stock at the time of order. A quotation is not an order; an order is confirmed only when we confirm it in writing or by invoice.

Warranty

Electric-vehicle batteries carry a 36-month warranty and inverter batteries a 60-month warranty from the invoice date, unless your invoice states otherwise. Keep your invoice and the serial number on the pack; both are needed for a claim.

The warranty does not cover damage from opening or modifying the pack, physical impact, water, fire, exposure to direct sunlight, or charging with a charger other than the recommended lithium charger. As printed on every battery label, no claim is accepted if the usage instructions are not followed.

Specifications and estimates

Specifications are from our technical sheets and product labels. Range, backup and savings shown by the website calculators are estimates for guidance; real results depend on load, road, temperature, charger and usage.
